BCA

Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) is a three-year undergraduate degree course focused on computer science and information technology.
The course provides knowledge of programming languages, database management, web development, software engineering and computer applications.
Students learn programming languages such as C, C++, Java and Python. They also gain practical knowledge through laboratory sessions and projects.
BCA helps students develop technical skills, logical thinking and problem-solving abilities.
After completing BCA, students can pursue careers in software development, web development, database administration and IT support.
Students can also continue their higher education by pursuing MCA (Master of Computer Applications).

Bsc

Bachelor of Science (B.Sc) is an undergraduate degree course focused on science, technology and research.
The course provides knowledge in subjects such as Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science.
Students gain theoretical knowledge as well as practical experience through laboratory sessions and experiments.
B.Sc helps students develop scientific thinking, analytical skills and problem-solving abilities.
After completing B.Sc, students can pursue careers in research, education, laboratories, healthcare and technology.
Students can also continue their higher education by pursuing M.Sc, MCA and other professional courses.

BCOM

Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) is an undergraduate degree course focused on commerce, business, finance and accounting.
The course provides knowledge of financial accounting, business management, economics, taxation and banking.
Students learn about business operations, financial management and the principles of commerce.
B.Com helps students develop analytical, communication and problem-solving skills.
After completing B.Com, students can pursue careers in accounting, banking, finance, taxation and business management.
Students can also continue their higher education by pursuing M.Com, MBA, CA and other professional courses.
